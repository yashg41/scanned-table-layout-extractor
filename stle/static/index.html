Scanned table layout extractor

Drop a PDF here, or choose one. Then drag a rectangle around a table and watch the layout come back out of the pixels.

Nothing leaves your machine. Files are stored beside the app, in uploads/.

Drag to mark a table. Arrow keys nudge the box, Alt+arrow resizes it, Shift for ten pixels. Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms the page; +/− and 0 (fit) work from the keyboard.
